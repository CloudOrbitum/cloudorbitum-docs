## Rate limits

| Plan | Requests per minute | Graph queries per minute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 120 | 20 |
| Team | 1,200 | 200 |
| Enterprise | 12,000 | 2,000 |

When you go over a limit, the API answers `429` with a `Retry-After` header in seconds.

## Quotas

| Resource | Free | Team | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Connected cloud accounts | 2 | 25 | Unlimited |
| Runbooks | 5 | 100 | Unlimited |
| Sandbox tenants | 0 | 2 | 10 |
| Audit log retention | 30 days | 400 days | 400 days |
