---
title: Sandbox tenants
description: Isolated copies of your estate's shape, for simulations and runbook tests.
content_type: how-to
products:
  - simulation-lab
---

A sandbox tenant mirrors the shape of your estate (resources, relationships, policies) without any of its data or access.

```sh
orb lab tenants create sandbox-1 --mirror acme-demo
orb lab tenants list
```

<Aside type="note">
  Sandbox tenants expire after 30 days unless you extend them with `orb lab tenants extend`.
</Aside>
