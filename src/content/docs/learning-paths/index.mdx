## Secure a multi-cloud estate

Six modules from a first exposure scan to closing attack paths through reviewed pull requests.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Map your estate">
    [Connect Azure](/connect/azure/) and [AWS](/connect/aws/), then open the live map.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find what is exposed">
    Run your first [graph query](/graph/) and read the exposure path of each result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put the edge in front">
    [Connect Cloudflare](/connect/cloudflare-edge/) and find origins that skip the edge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get told when it changes">
    Send [alerts](/alerts/) to your on-call tool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let an agent propose the fix">
    Give Warden a mission and review its proposal in [approvals](/agents/approvals/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rehearse the next incident">
    Replay a real incident in the [Simulation lab](/simulation-lab/).
  </Step>
</Steps>
